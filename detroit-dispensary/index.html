Our Detroit dispensary

House of Zen Detroit

A licensed, family-run cannabis dispensary on Mack Ave. In the words of our live menu, we are “your hidden gem located on the eastside of Detroit.”

Store details

You will find us at 14501 Mack Ave, Detroit, MI 48215. Our hours are Mon-Sun: 9 am to 9 pm. You can call (313) 332-1733 or email hello@houseofzendetroit.com. House of Zen is for adults 21 and older.

We are one store, family-owned and family-run, with Teri Hargrave and Jacquline Weathersby as owners.

Would you like to order ahead? Our live menu lists in-store and curbside pickup. Please have your photo ID with you when you pick up.

Visiting House of Zen

What to bring

A valid driver’s license or government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. In Michigan, a licensed adult-use store must check it before any sale. If you hold a medical card, please call us to confirm whether and how we can serve medical card holders today.

What to expect

Come as you are, and take your time. Whether this is your first visit or one of many, our team is here to guide you through:

  • Product education tailored to your needs
  • Clear, honest recommendations
  • Easy, guided checkout experience
  • A judgment-free environment

New here? Our Dispensary Guide shows how a first visit goes, and our welcome is simple: first customer deals: 30% OFF the purchase. Please see Deals for the terms that may apply.

What we carry

We carry a full selection of licensed cannabis products designed to meet every lifestyle and experience level.

Our categories are Premium Flower, Pre Rolls, Edibles, Concentrates, Topicals, CBD Products, Vaporizers and Accessories.

All products are fully licensed, lab-tested, and compliant with state regulations. For what is available today, please see our live menu, or ask us when you come in.
