How old do I need to be, and what ID do I bring?
You need to be 21 or older, and Michigan requires a licensed adult-use store to check a valid driver’s license or government-issued photo ID before any sale. If you hold a medical card, please call us to confirm whether and how we can serve medical card holders today.

Cannabis basics, Michigan

Where can I use cannabis?
Using marijuana in a public place is not allowed in Michigan, and property owners may ban smoking or use on their property. Michigan Cannabis Laws has more, including areas a municipality may designate for consumption.
How much can I buy?
A licensed adult-use store may sell no more than 2.5 ounces in a single transaction, with no more than 15 grams of it as concentrate. Adults 21 and older may carry up to that same amount.
Why does the price change at checkout?
Michigan adds a 10% excise tax and a 6% sales tax to adult-use purchases, and the sales tax is figured on the price including the excise. On a $100 sale, that comes to $10 of excise tax and $6.60 of sales tax.
What if I am new?
Start with a small amount, give it plenty of time, and only then decide whether you want more. Everyone responds differently, so there is no hurry, and our Dispensary Guide and Edibles: Start Low, Wait Long are good places to begin.
Michigan details are as of October 2026. General information, not legal advice.
