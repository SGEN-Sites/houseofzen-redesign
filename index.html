about House of Zen

A Family-Owned Dispensary Built on Trust

House of Zen is a licensed, family-run cannabis dispensary rooted in care, community, and education. We believe in creating a safe, welcoming space where customers can learn, explore, and feel confident in every choice they make.

Our team is committed to providing a personalized experience through knowledgeable guidance, quality products, and approachable support for both new and experienced consumers. At House of Zen, we focus on building genuine connections within the community while promoting responsible, informed cannabis use in a comfortable and inclusive environment.
