Concentrates

Concentrates

Concentrates are cannabis extracts that hold a lot in a small amount. Most people come to them after some time with other formats.

Shop Concentrates

What it is

A concentrate separates the resin of the plant from the rest of it. You may hear names such as wax, shatter, rosin or live resin, which describe its texture and how it was made. Most are used with a dedicated device, so ask us what you will need.

How people choose

People choose by texture, by how it was made and by what the label lists for THC and CBD. Michigan lets adults 21 and older carry up to 2.5 ounces of marijuana, of which no more than 15 grams may be concentrate. If you are new to this format, tell us, and we will slow down and explain.

Worth knowing

Tell us what you have tried so far, and we will explain the differences before we suggest anything.

Questions

Are concentrates a good place to start?
They can be a lot for someone new, so many people begin with another format first. Tell us your comfort level, and we will talk it through.
