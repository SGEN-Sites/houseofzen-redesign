Concept

Rewards

Families do not keep score, but they do remember you. Here is a sketch of how that feeling could become a rewards program.

Concept for discussion: House of Zen has not announced a rewards program. This page shows how one could look, with terms to be set by the owners.

Share my interest

Why rewards could fit a family dispensary

House of Zen began as a family vision, and we think of every customer as family. A rewards program, if the owners choose to build one, could grow out of that: a way of saying we are glad you keep coming back.

Many programs begin with arithmetic. This one could begin with something else: knowing your name, remembering what you liked, and taking the time to explain what is new.

This is only a sketch. There is no program to join today, nothing to earn, and nothing on this page is a promise.

A sketch

How it could work

  1. 1

    Join

    You could sign up at the counter or on this site, with nothing more than a first name and an email address.
  2. 2

    Visit

    You could shop the way you always do, with no target to reach and no pressure to buy more than you came for.
  3. 3

    Be remembered

    If you would like, our team could keep a note of what you have enjoyed and what you would rather skip, so each conversation picks up where the last one ended.

Ideas, not offers

Ideas to shape together

None of these exist today. They are places to begin a conversation.

  • Idea: a quiet note when something new arrives in a category you told us you like.
  • Idea: a small thank-you from the family on a day that matters to you.
  • Idea: an education evening at the store, where you can ask the questions you have been saving up.
  • Idea: a word of thanks for introducing a friend who is twenty-one or older to the house.
  • Idea: a way to pass your thank-you along to a community cause chosen by the family.

Tell us which of these sound right and which do not. If a program is ever built, we would want it to sound like this family and like you.

The owners would set any terms, rewards and eligibility. Our Terms of Use already note that loyalty programs may be subject to additional terms and conditions.
